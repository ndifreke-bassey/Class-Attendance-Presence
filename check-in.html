Terms and data use

Please review how your information is used for this class check-in.

What this check-in uses

  • Your registration number is checked against this class roster. Your name and programme are shown so you can confirm the correct student profile.
  • Your device location and its accuracy are used to verify that you are near the class venue. The app records the rounded distance, not your raw coordinates.
  • If you submit attendance, the app stores your student and class details, the check-in time, and the attendance result for the lecturer to review.
  • A camera photo is captured on your device only. It is not uploaded or saved, and face identity matching is not enabled.

Location and camera access are requested separately by your browser. You can decline either permission, but attendance cannot be submitted without the required checks. Only use this link for your own check-in.
